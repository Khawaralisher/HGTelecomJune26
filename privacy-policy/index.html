This Privacy Policy explains how HG TeleCommunication (Pvt) Ltd (“HG”, “we”, “us”) handles personal information when you visit hg.com.pk, contact us, apply for a role, or use services we provide as a licensed Long Distance International (LDI) operator in Pakistan.

It applies to this website and to the personal information we handle as a company. Services delivered under a signed commercial agreement may also be governed by data-protection terms in that agreement. Where those terms conflict with this policy, the signed agreement takes precedence.

1. Who we are

HG TeleCommunication (Pvt) Ltd is a private limited company incorporated in Pakistan in 2022 and licensed by the Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA) as an LDI operator since 2023. We are the data controller for the information described in this policy.

Registered address: Office 209B, 2nd Floor, Evacuee Trust, Islamabad, Pakistan.
Email: info@hg.com.pk  ·  Telephone: +92 51 2726552

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Your name, email address and the content of your message when you submit an enquiry form on this website.
  • Information you choose to include when you contact us about a career opportunity, including anything contained in a CV or covering message you send us.
  • Business contact details and commercial information exchanged when you engage with us as a carrier, enterprise or wholesale customer.

Information collected automatically

  • Standard web server logs, which may include your IP address, browser type, the pages you requested and the date and time of the request.
  • Information stored by strictly necessary cookies. See section 8.

Information arising from our licensed services

As a licensed operator we process traffic and signalling data necessary to route, terminate and bill international voice, data and messaging traffic. This includes call detail records containing originating and terminating numbers, timestamps, duration and routing information. We do not listen to, record or read the content of communications carried across our network except where we are lawfully required to do so.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide the information or service you asked for.
  • To provide, maintain, monitor and bill for our services, and to manage our relationship with customers, carriers and suppliers.
  • To operate, secure and troubleshoot our network, including fraud detection and prevention.
  • To assess applications for employment.
  • To meet our legal, regulatory and licence obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use the details you submit through this website to send you marketing you did not ask for.

4. Legal basis and regulatory obligations

We process personal information where it is necessary to perform a contract with you, where we have a legitimate business interest in operating and securing our network, where you have given consent, and where we are required to by law.

As a PTA licensee we operate under the Pakistan Telecommunication (Re-organization) Act 1996, the conditions of our licence, the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act 2016 and directions issued by the PTA and other competent authorities. These may require us to retain certain records and to disclose information to authorised agencies. Where we receive such a request we satisfy ourselves that it is lawful and properly authorised before responding.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We share personal information only where necessary, and only with:

  • Interconnecting carriers and network partners, to the extent required to route and terminate traffic.
  • Service providers who support our business, such as hosting, connectivity and professional advisers, acting on our instructions.
  • The PTA, law-enforcement agencies, courts and other authorities, where we are lawfully required to disclose.
  • A purchaser or successor, in the event of a reorganisation, merger or sale of the business.

6. Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as it is needed for the purpose it was collected for, or for as long as we are required to keep it. Website enquiries are retained for as long as needed to deal with the enquiry and for a reasonable period afterwards. Records that we are obliged to retain under our licence, applicable regulations or PTA direction are kept for the period those obligations specify. When information is no longer required, we delete it or render it unreadable.

7. Security

We maintain technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including network security controls, restricted access to systems holding personal information, and monitoring of our infrastructure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach occurs that is likely to affect you, we will act in line with our regulatory obligations.

8. Cookies and website analytics

This website uses only cookies and local browser storage that are strictly necessary for the site to function. We do not use advertising cookies and we do not track you across other websites. If we introduce analytics or other non-essential cookies in future, we will update this policy and seek your consent where required.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to:

  • Confirm what personal information we hold about you and provide a copy.
  • Correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Delete information we no longer have a lawful reason to keep.
  • Restrict or object to a particular use of your information.
  • Withdraw a consent you previously gave, without affecting processing already carried out.

Write to info@hg.com.pk and we will respond within a reasonable period. We may need to verify your identity first. Some rights do not apply where we are legally obliged to retain the information.

10. International transfers

We carry international traffic, and some of our carrier partners, suppliers and systems are located outside Pakistan. Where personal information is transferred outside Pakistan, we take reasonable steps to ensure it remains protected to a standard consistent with this policy and with applicable Pakistani law.

11. Children

This website and our services are directed at businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to our services, our systems or the law. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. Material changes will be highlighted on this page.
